COMMON VECTOR SPACES

One structure, many kinds of objects

Vectors do not have to be arrows. Any collection with valid addition and scalar multiplication can form a vector space over a field 𝔽.

01 · TUPLES
𝔽n

Ordered n-tuples

The vector space of ordered n-tuples whose entries come from 𝔽.

(a₁, a₂, …, aₙ)
02 · MATRICES
ℳn×m(𝔽)

n × m matrices

Matrices over 𝔽, added entry by entry and scaled by one field element.

[aᵢⱼ] + [bᵢⱼ] = [aᵢⱼ + bᵢⱼ]
03 · POLYNOMIALS
𝔽[X]

Polynomials over 𝔽

Polynomials of finite degree whose coefficients are elements of 𝔽.

a₀ + a₁X + ⋯ + aₖXᵏ
04 · FUNCTIONS
𝔽𝔽

Functions from 𝔽 to 𝔽

All functions with pointwise addition and scalar multiplication.
